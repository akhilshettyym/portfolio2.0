What you're describing is not a portfolio.
It's a narrative experience.
The visitor shouldn't feel like they're reading sections such as "About", "Skills", "Experience", and "Contact".
They should feel like they're uncovering a person.
The best storytelling portfolios create curiosity first, credibility second, and only then sell expertise.
Right now your blueprint has strong ideas, but it's jumping too quickly between concepts. The story needs emotional progression.

Story Structure
Think of it as a movie.
Act 1 — Curiosity
Make people ask:
Who is this guy?
before answering it.
Act 2 — Discovery
Reveal your thinking, skills, and journey.
Act 3 — Authority
Show proof that you're the person who can solve difficult problems.
Act 4 — Identity
End with a memorable statement.
